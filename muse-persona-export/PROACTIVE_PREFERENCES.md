# PROACTIVE_PREFERENCES.md

_What you want to hear about without asking, what you never want brought up, and when. Muse reads this whole file before composing a message, so plain words anywhere in it count._

## Tell me about

- Proactive nudges are welcome when they arrive as one concrete action.

## Never tell me about

- Never repeat a nudge he ignored.

## When

_Messages may arrive from 09:00 to 21:30 local time. Timing preferences guide composition; delivery may wait for a quiet moment._

## How

- Rank information hard, always offer one clear next step, keep pings short.
- Draft. Do not send. — drafts are held and nothing goes out without his explicit instruction.
- Format wishes: one brief line, a card stack, a full page when it needs one, a particular tone.
